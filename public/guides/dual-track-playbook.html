Dual-Track Playbook: STAKING vs BUY/HOLD

Two different decisions on the same TAO. Track 1 is a yield decision — it reads validators, APY, commission and risk. Track 2 is a price-direction decision — it reads TAO price, entry zones, DCA and volatility. Never let one answer the other's question.

Snapshot 2026-11-06 Network 129 active subnets TAO ~$220–240 Ecosystem revenue ~$70M annualized, +40% m/m
The one rule: in dTAO, staking into a subnet and buying its alpha token are the same on-chain action (TAO ↔ alpha via the subnet liquidity pool). So separate the two questions first — “which subnet earns the best sustainable yield?” (Track 1) and “is TAO itself a good price to accumulate?” (Track 2) — then combine them at allocation time.

Take can only be raised once per ~30 days and never lowered — so check the live value before delegating, not after:

btcli query delegate-take --hotkey 5F...

Root payouts also depend on the validator's VTrust (cross-subnet validation trust) — high stake share with low VTrust earns less from root.
